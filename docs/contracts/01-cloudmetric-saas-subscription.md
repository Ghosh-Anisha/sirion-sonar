This Agreement is made on 1 May 2026 between Cloudmetric Software Private Limited ("Provider") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Customer").

1. Subscription. Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive right to use the Cloudmetric analytics platform for up to 150 named users.
2. Term. The subscription starts on 1 May 2026 and runs for twelve (12) months, ending on 30 April 2027 (the "Initial Term").
3. Renewal. After the Initial Term the subscription renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the current term.
4. Fees. The annual fee is Rs 18,00,000, invoiced yearly in advance and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.
5. Price changes. Provider may raise the fee for a renewal term by no more than seven percent (7%), and only if it tells Customer in writing at least ninety (90) days before the current term ends.
6. Availability. Provider will keep the platform available 99.5% of each month, excluding planned maintenance. If availability falls below that level, Customer receives a credit of 5% of the monthly fee for each full percentage point missed.
7. Support. Provider answers critical issues within four (4) business hours and other issues within two (2) business days.
8. Termination. Either party may end this Agreement for an uncured material breach on thirty (30) days' written notice.
9. Data. Customer owns its data. On termination Provider returns it within thirty (30) days and then deletes it.
10. Law. Indian law applies. Courts in Bengaluru have jurisdiction.
