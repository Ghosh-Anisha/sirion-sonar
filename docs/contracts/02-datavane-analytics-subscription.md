This Agreement is made on 1 September 2025 between Datavane Analytics Private Limited ("Datavane") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Customer").

1. Services. Datavane provides the Datavane reporting suite described in Order Form 1, attached.
2. Initial term. The first subscription term is twenty-four (24) months from 1 September 2025 and ends on 31 August 2027.
3. Automatic renewal. Unless a party ends the subscription by written notice three (3) months before the end of a term, the subscription renews for a further twelve (12) months.
4. Fees. The fee is Rs 9,60,000 a year, billed annually in advance. Invoices are due in forty-five (45) days.
5. Price on renewal. The renewal price equals the price of the prior term unless Datavane gives written notice of an increase at least ninety (90) days before the end of that term.
6. Support. Support is provided on business days from 9 am to 6 pm IST. Datavane responds to urgent requests within one (1) business day.
7. Termination for cause. Either party may terminate for a material breach not fixed within thirty (30) days of written notice.
8. Confidentiality. Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.
9. Law. Indian law applies.
