This Agreement is made on 1 July 2025 between Brightline Logistics Private Limited ("Carrier") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Shipper").

1. Services. Carrier moves the Shipper's goods between its plants and distribution centres under shipment orders placed through the Carrier portal.
2. Term. The Agreement runs for twenty-four (24) months from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2027.
3. Renewal. It then renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice at least ninety (90) days before the end of the current term.
4. Rates. The agreed rate is Rs 42 per kilometre for a 10 tonne truck and Rs 58 per kilometre for a 20 tonne truck. Waiting time beyond four (4) hours is charged at Rs 650 per hour.
5. Fuel surcharge. Carrier may add a fuel surcharge that tracks the published diesel price, capped at six percent (6%) of the freight charge.
6. Invoices. Carrier invoices monthly. An invoice must list each shipment, the distance and the rate applied. Shipper pays within forty-five (45) days of receiving a correct invoice and may withhold a disputed amount.
7. Service level. Carrier delivers at least ninety-five percent (95%) of shipments by the committed delivery date each month.
8. Service credits. For each full percentage point below the service level, Carrier credits Shipper two percent (2%) of that month's freight charges, up to a maximum of twenty percent (20%).
9. Damage. Carrier is liable for loss or damage in transit up to the invoice value of the goods.
10. Termination. Either party may terminate on sixty (60) days' notice if the service level is missed in three consecutive months.
11. Law. Indian law applies.
