This Agreement is made on 1 February 2026 between Swiftlane Freight Private Limited ("Swiftlane") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Customer").

1. Scope. Swiftlane carries parcels and pallets on the express lanes listed in Schedule A.
2. Term. This Agreement runs from 1 February 2026 for twelve (12) months and ends on 31 January 2027.
3. Renewal. It renews for a further twelve (12) months unless a party gives thirty (30) days' written notice before the end of the term.
4. Rates. Express lane freight is charged at Rs 3.10 per kilogram, with a minimum of Rs 450 per consignment.
5. Delivery. Swiftlane delivers express consignments within two (2) business days of pickup. For each late consignment Customer receives a credit of ten percent (10%) of that consignment's charge.
6. Payment. Invoices are monthly and due in thirty (30) days.
7. Claims. Customer reports damage or loss within seven (7) days of delivery.
8. Termination. Either party may end this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice for a material breach left unfixed.
