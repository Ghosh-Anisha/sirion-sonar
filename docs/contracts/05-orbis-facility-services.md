This Agreement is made on 2 April 2024 between Orbis Facility Services Private Limited ("Contractor") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Client").

1. Services. Contractor provides housekeeping, electrical and plumbing maintenance and pantry services at the Client's head office in Gurugram, as listed in Schedule 1.
2. Term. The term is three (3) years from 2 April 2024 to 1 April 2027.
3. Renewal. The Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice before the end of the current term.
4. Fees. The monthly fee is Rs 4,20,000, invoiced at the start of each month and payable in thirty (30) days.
5. Annual review. The fee may be revised once a year by agreement, and by no more than five percent (5%) without Client's written consent.
6. Service levels. Contractor attends urgent faults within two (2) hours and routine requests within one (1) business day. Missed response times in a month are listed in the monthly report.
7. Staff. Contractor supplies trained staff and is responsible for their wages, insurance and statutory payments.
8. Termination. Client may terminate for repeated service failures (four or more missed urgent responses in a quarter) on thirty (30) days' notice.
9. Law. Indian law applies.
