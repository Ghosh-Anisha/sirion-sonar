1. Term. Clause 2 is replaced with: The term runs until 31 March 2028.
2. Notice. In clause 3, ninety (90) days is replaced with one hundred and twenty (120) days.
3. Fees. In clause 4, the monthly fee of Rs 4,20,000 is replaced with Rs 4,41,000 from 1 December 2026.
4. Other terms. Every other term of the Agreement stays as it is.
5. Counterparts. This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
