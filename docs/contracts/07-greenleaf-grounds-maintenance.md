This Agreement is made on 1 January 2025 between Greenleaf Landscaping LLP ("Contractor") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Client").

1. Services. Contractor maintains the lawns, trees and paths at the Client's three campuses listed in Schedule A.
2. Term. The first term is one (1) year from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025.
3. Renewal. The Client may renew the Agreement for a further year, up to four (4) times, by written confirmation given at least thirty (30) days before the end of the current year. If the Client gives no confirmation, the Agreement ends.
4. Fee. The annual fee is Rs 6,00,000, paid in four equal quarterly instalments.
5. Standards. Contractor visits each campus twice a week and keeps a visit log.
6. Termination. Client may terminate at any time on sixty (60) days' written notice.
