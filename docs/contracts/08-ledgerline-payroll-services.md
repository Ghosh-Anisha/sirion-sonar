This Agreement is made on 1 January 2026 between Ledgerline Payroll Services Private Limited ("Ledgerline") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Client").

1. Services. Ledgerline runs the Client's monthly payroll, prepares statutory returns and issues payslips.
2. Term. The Agreement runs for one (1) year ending on 31 December 2026.
3. Renewal. It renews for further terms of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice thirty (30) days before the end of the current term.
4. Fees. The service fee is Rs 65,000 a month, invoiced monthly and due in fifteen (15) days.
5. Increases. Ledgerline may increase the fee for a renewal term by no more than five percent (5%), and only if it gives written notice at least sixty (60) days before the end of the current term.
6. Accuracy. If an error by Ledgerline causes a late filing, Ledgerline pays the resulting penalty.
7. Data. Ledgerline keeps employee data confidential and deletes it on request after termination.
8. Termination. Either party may terminate for uncured material breach on thirty (30) days' notice.
