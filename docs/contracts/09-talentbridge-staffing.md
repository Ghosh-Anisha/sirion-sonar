This Agreement is made on 1 July 2026 between Talentbridge Staffing Private Limited ("Agency") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Client").

1. Services. Agency supplies temporary workers for warehouse, data entry and customer support roles on written requests from the Client.
2. Term. The Agreement runs for twelve (12) months from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
3. Renewal. It renews for further twelve (12) month terms unless a party gives written notice forty-five (45) days before the end of the term.
4. Charges. Client pays Agency the worker's pay cost plus a markup of eighteen percent (18%). Agency invoices every two weeks against signed timesheets.
5. Employer duties. Agency is the employer of each worker and handles wages, insurance and statutory payments.
6. Replacement. Agency replaces an unsuitable worker within three (3) business days of a request, at no extra charge.
7. Conversion. If Client hires an Agency worker within six (6) months of placement, Client pays a conversion fee of 8.33% of the worker's annual pay.
8. Termination. Either party may terminate on thirty (30) days' notice for uncured material breach.
