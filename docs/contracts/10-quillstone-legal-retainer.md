This Agreement is made on 1 January 2026 between Quillstone Legal LLP ("Firm") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Client").

1. Services. Firm provides general commercial legal advice, up to forty (40) hours a month.
2. Term. The retainer runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026.
3. Renewal. It renews for a further twelve (12) months unless either party gives twenty-one (21) days' written notice before the end of the term.
4. Fees. Client pays a monthly retainer of Rs 2,50,000. Hours above forty (40) in a month are charged at Rs 7,500 per hour.
5. Conflicts. Firm will tell Client promptly of any conflict of interest.
6. Termination. Either party may end the retainer on thirty (30) days' written notice.
