This Master Supply Agreement is made on 12 August 2025 between Pinecrest Industrial Supplies Private Limited ("Supplier") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Buyer").

1. Supply. Supplier sells packaging materials and spare parts to Buyer on Order Forms signed by both parties.
2. Term. The term of each supply arrangement, any renewal and any notice period are set out in the applicable Order Form.
3. Prices. Prices are listed in the applicable Order Form and are fixed for its term.
4. Delivery. Supplier delivers within the lead time stated in the Order Form.
5. Payment. Buyer pays undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days.
6. Quality. Buyer may reject goods that do not meet the specification within ten (10) days of delivery.
7. Order Form 1. Attached as Schedule 1.
