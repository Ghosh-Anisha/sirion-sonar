This Agreement is made on 1 September 2026 between Arjun Rao ("Consultant") and Halden Foods Private Limited ("Client").

1. Services. Consultant reviews the Client's warehouse process and delivers a written recommendation report.
2. Term. The Agreement runs for six (6) months, from 1 September 2026 to 28 February 2027, and does not renew automatically.
3. Fees. Client pays Rs 1,80,000 on each of three milestones: kickoff, draft report and final report.
4. Independence. Consultant works as an independent contractor and not as an employee.
5. Confidentiality. Consultant keeps the Client's information confidential during and after the Agreement.
6. Ownership. Client owns the final report.
7. Termination. Either party may end the Agreement on fifteen (15) days' notice, and Client pays for work done to that date.
